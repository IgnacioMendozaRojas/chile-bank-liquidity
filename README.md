# chile-bank-liquidity

**Liquidity risk and deposit behaviour of Chilean banks**, from public CMF balance sheets and Banco Central data: how stable each bank's deposits are, how much could leave under stress, and how long liquid assets would last.

Work in progress. Step 1, the data layer, is in place: monthly balance sheets of every bank since 2008 from the CMF API, mapped across the 2022 change in the chart of accounts, checked against the Banco Central's monetary aggregates, with mergers detected from the data and folded into pro-forma histories.
